# Track-And-Field
NES Track and Field made in Godot
<br>
<br>So in the Olympics the 100 meter dash (our first event) the world record is 9.63 seconds. 
<br>So for the game I think it would be cool to see this as just about impossible but still able to get. 
<br>Then maybe we track the personal record so they have something to beat. 
<br>
<br>I’m the NES version there is a time of 14 seconds that you need to beat in order to move on to the next round. 
<br>If you are slower that person doesn’t move on to the next event. 
<br>I would like to allow two options. The traditional way like this but also a 10 competition version that you play all 10 and the most points at the end wins.
<br>
<br>The long jump world record is 8.95 meters. So we should keep to a similar way that it’s very very hard to get. In the NES version you need to beat 5.5 meters.
<br>
<br>For the 110 meter hurdles there are 10 hurdles to jump and if you hit a hurdle your person gets slowed down a bit or sometimes falls down. The qualifying time to beat is 15.5 seconds.




The record for hurdles is 12.8 seconds.
